Frequently Asked Questions About UNN

When was UNN founded?

UNN was founded in 1960 and is known as Nigeria's first full fledged indigenous university.

How many campuses does UNN have?

UNN operates across three campuses which are Nsukka, Enugu, and Ituku Ozalla.

How many faculties and departments are in UNN?

The university has 17 faculties and over 108 departments.

What is the student population?

UNN has more than 36,000 students across its campuses.

Where can students find academic information?

Students can access academic calendars, course information, and schedules through the official UNN student portal.
